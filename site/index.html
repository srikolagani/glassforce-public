Already in the panel

Five things you can do in the app today.

Keyboard-first

Type, move with the arrow keys, and press Return. Esc dismisses the panel.

Fuzzy search stays under 50ms

A misspelled name still finds the record.

Open the record in Lightning

The row you pick opens in Lightning, in your browser.

Choose which objects are searchable

The built-in set is Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Cases, Reports, and Setup pages. You can add custom objects, and you can turn an object off.

Privacy

You connect with OAuth PKCE. Tokens stay in the Keychain. Glassforce sends no telemetry. Salesforce data stays on the Mac.

A developer org

Scripted mock demonstration from commit eae6d2a. Synthetic data. The chip in the picture says Sandbox · Acme Corp (UAT), not Demo. The preview host is 127.0.0.1. Not a production org, and not proof of later preview spacing or Demo labeling. Shots that showed a real org are not embedded.

Live today

The hotkey panel, fuzzy search, open record, the object picker, and OAuth connect are in the app today.
